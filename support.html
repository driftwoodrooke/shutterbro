How ShutterBro works

ShutterBro helps you compose better photographs using a reference image overlay.

  • Select a reference image from your Photos library.
  • Use the transparent overlay to help frame your shot.
  • Capture your photograph with the camera.
  • Save your finished photograph to your Photos library.

Camera and Photos Access

ShutterBro uses camera access to capture photographs and Photos access to select reference images and save your finished photographs.
