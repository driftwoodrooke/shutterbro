How ShutterBro works

ShutterBro helps you compose photographs using a reference image overlay.

  • Select a reference image from your Photos library.
  • Use the image overlay to help frame your photograph.
  • Capture your photograph using the camera.
  • Save your finished photograph to your Photos library.

Camera and Photos Access

ShutterBro needs camera access to take photographs and Photos access to select reference images and save your photographs.
