Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Introduction

ShutterBro is a simple camera app designed to help you compose better photographs using a reference image overlay.

Your privacy matters. ShutterBro is designed to operate on your device without collecting or transmitting your personal data.

Camera Access

ShutterBro uses your device's camera to capture photographs. Camera access is used only when you use the camera functionality within the app.

Photos Access

ShutterBro allows you to select a reference image from your Photos library and save captured photographs to your library. These images are processed locally on your device.

Information We Collect

ShutterBro does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. We do not operate user accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking services within the app.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties.

Photos stored in your Photos library remain subject to your own device and Apple account settings, including any iCloud Photos synchronisation you have enabled.

Data Security

ShutterBro processes camera images and selected reference photos locally on your device. We do not maintain a remote database of user information.

Children's Privacy

ShutterBro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ShutterBro, please contact us through the developer contact information provided on the app's App Store page.
